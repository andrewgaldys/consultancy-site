One Client at a Time

Our commitment to every business we serve

Most agencies and consulting firms grow by adding accounts. Each new client gets a smaller share of the team’s time, and the work tends to go to whoever happens to be free that week.

Patriot Consulting works another way. We take on one client at a time, and while we are working with you, yours is the only business we are building. Every hour we spend goes to your project.

That arrangement keeps us accountable. You always know who is doing the work, and you never wait behind another client. Each system we put in place is tested and written down before we hand it over, so your team can keep it running long after the engagement ends.

The Four Pillars of Implementation

When you hire us to carry out the work, it falls into four areas.

I. Foundation & Administration

We handle the administrative steps of forming your LLC and obtaining an EIN, prepare a compliance checklist suited to your organization, and put your business accounts in order.

II. Financial Workflows

We set up your bookkeeping, separate your personal and business finances, and put simple cash-flow tracking in place so you can see where the money goes each month.

III. Operations & Systems

We set up a CRM, automate how new clients are brought on, organize your scheduling, and write standard operating procedures so the daily work is done the same way every time.

IV. Growth & Acquisition

We give you a clean web presence, make you easier to find locally, and run targeted digital advertising designed to bring steady clients through the door.

How We Work

Three steps. The first two cost you nothing.

  1. I.Intro

    No charge

    A fifteen-minute phone call. We learn about your mission, your goals, and where things are getting stuck. There is nothing to prepare.

  2. II.Audit

    No charge, always

    A review of how your business runs today, from your books and accounts to your scheduling, systems, and marketing. We use modern AI tools to complete it about ten times faster than a traditional firm, and you receive a written roadmap that sets out what to fix and in what order. The roadmap is yours to keep whether or not you hire us.

  3. III.Implement

    Paid engagement

    If you would like us to carry out the roadmap, you become our only active client while we build and install the systems we agreed on. Scope and price are set in writing before any work begins.

Why the Audit Is Free

Most firms charge for this kind of review because it takes them weeks. With AI tools handling much of the sorting and analysis, it takes us a fraction of that time. Since it costs us so little, we don’t think it right to charge anyone for it, least of all the veteran-owned businesses and community organizations we serve. The audit is free and will stay free. You pay only if you hire us for the implementation work.
